AI That Understands Civic Problems

CivicAI uses Scikit-Learn NLP models trained on real civic datasets to classify issues, detect emergency hazards, prevent duplicate work, and route to the best field squad.

🧠

1. NLP Issue Classification

Trained Scikit-Learn TF-IDF vectorizer + Multinomial Naive Bayes categorizes raw citizen text into 12 civic & campus domains with 95%+ precision.

TF-IDF + Naive Bayes
⚠️

2. Hazard & Urgency Scoring

Heuristic hazard keyword weighting detects electric shock, gas leaks, open manholes, and student safety hazards to automatically escalate to Critical status.

Life-Safety Heuristics
🔍

3. Spatial & Semantic Duplicate Detector

Haversine formula flags identical complaints within 150 meters and calculates cosine similarity on problem descriptions, stopping spam and duplicate tickets.

Haversine + Cosine Sim
🚜

4. Workload-Balanced Field Dispatch

Matches domain specialization (Roads, Drainage, Electrical, Campus) and balances queue capacity across 6 field maintenance squads for quickest SLA.

Workload Balancer
📊

5. Hotspot & Infrastructure Insights

Aggregates municipal ward density and campus incident clusters to generate predictive maintenance advisories before seasonal monsoons.

Spatial Density Clustering
